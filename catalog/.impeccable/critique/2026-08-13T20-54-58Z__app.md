## Priority Issues

**[P0] Silent Qurator rerouting in the header bar** — State.tsx:84-98 + classify.ts:24-38; ContentBar renders no route indicator; the highlighted suggestion row says "Search X" while Enter opens the assistant. Why: betrayal at the highest-frequency interaction; power users' ≥5-word keyword queries hijacked. Fix: surface the route pre-commit (FrontDoor badge idiom inside the header field, or flip row 0 to "Ask Qurator: …"); Enter must never override the visibly selected row. Command: /impeccable clarify + /impeccable polish.

**[P1] Fake "interpreted plan"** — extractCriteria.ts:11-32 hardcoded table; panel says "Interpreted as — edit before running" but chips aren't editable; "Claude · Bedrock · your permissions" + "auto-routed" tags assert trust. Why: a fabricated readout at the moment reassurance matters most; caught once, it taxes every readout. Fix: cut simulated criteria (query + destination + Run/Just-search only); delete "edit before running"; one quiet provenance line. Command: /impeccable distill.

**[P2] Display type in app states** — Empty.tsx:57 and NoResults.tsx:255 use h4 (2.125rem) against the No-Display-Font Rule; fontWeight 300 inline at List/Hit.tsx:227 (banned weight). Plus B's five off-ramp sizes. Fix: h5 for state headings; regular weight secondary ink; route the five sizes to tokens. Command: /impeccable typeset.

**[P2] Machine identity as prose in search hits** — List/Hit.tsx:219-222 hash truncated in body type, tooltip only, no copy affordance; handle heading also body type. The scientist's verification moment violates the Mono Identity Rule. Fix: mono face for hash + handle; add copy affordance. Command: /impeccable polish.

**[P2] One control, two shapes** — FrontDoor pill (radius 34, circular send, h6 text) vs ratified header field; pill focus = 1px border shift (violates 2px Focus Ring Rule) and the amber route border is overridden by the focus border exactly while typing. Fix: ratify the pill in DESIGN.md with a real focus treatment, or converge onto the header anatomy scaled up. Blocked on the two-bar decision. Command: /impeccable polish (after the decision).
